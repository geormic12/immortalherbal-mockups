Valerian Valeriana officinalis

Trials suggest it may shorten the time to fall asleep. Effects build over 2-4 weeks.

Dose: 0.3-3 g in a cup of hot water at bedtime (EMA monograph, sleep disorders)

Can cause drowsiness. Avoid with alcohol or sedatives.
